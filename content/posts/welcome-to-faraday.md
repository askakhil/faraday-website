## What is Faraday?

Faraday is a free, open-source toolkit of web apps that run in your car's browser.

It is named after Michael Faraday, whose work made electric motors possible. And it sounds like "far a day" — the question every electric driver asks: how far can I go in a day?

## The first app

**Signal Predictor** learns traffic signal cycles from your own drives, then predicts the countdown to green.

- [Try the live demo](https://tesla-signal-app.vercel.app)
- [See it on GitHub](https://github.com/akhilsaikoppanatham/tesla-signal-app)

## What comes next

More apps in the same clean, iOS-like style are coming soon — charge and trip tracking, parking, road-trip planning, and much more. See the full list on the [Work page](/work).

## The rules

- Free, always. No subscriptions, no paid add-ons.
- Browser only. No downloads, no app store.
- Your data stays in your browser unless you choose to share it.
